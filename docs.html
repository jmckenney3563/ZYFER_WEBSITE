Welcome to ZYFER

ZYFER is a full-stack cybersecurity platform for detecting, analyzing, and mitigating AI-generated impersonation attacks using synthetic forensics, with real-time processing, zero-trust architecture, and enterprise governance.

ZYFER lets security teams and enterprises protect their communications infrastructure from deepfake voice, video, and behavioral manipulation attacks using advanced multimodal AI. You describe your security requirements, and ZYFER deploys a protective layer that includes real-time detection, automated response, forensic logging, and integration capabilities, all backed by explainable AI and editable configurations.

ZYFER supports the full lifecycle of modern cybersecurity operations, from initial threat assessment and pilot deployment to enterprise-wide rollout and ongoing threat intelligence updates. Applications are protected through shared security workspaces, where teams collaborate across one or more detection instances. Each deployment represents a single protective layer and produces forensic logs that can be synced to SIEM platforms and integrated into existing security workflows. The platform is built to help security teams move faster while maintaining situational awareness, operational control, and digital trust.

Enterprises

What you can detect with ZYFER

ZYFER supports a broad range of synthetic threats, from common deepfakes to advanced behavioral manipulation.

Voice synthesis and audio deepfakes
Real-time cloning detection, text-to-speech artifacts, and voice conversion attacks in VoIP and conferencing platforms

Video and facial manipulation
Face-swap detection, lip-sync inconsistency analysis, and generative video artifacts in recorded and live streams

Behavioral biometrics and synthetic interactions
Keystroke injection detection, unnatural mouse movement patterns, and automated agent detection in collaboration tools

Cross-modal inconsistency detection
Voice-to-video synchronization analysis, temporal consistency verification, and multi-factor anomaly correlation

Real-time communication protection
Live streaming analysis, webinar protection, and meeting platform integration for immediate threat blocking

Forensic analysis and evidence preservation
Deepfake sample archiving, chain-of-custody logging, and court-admissible forensic reporting for legal proceedings

Security, privacy, and compliance

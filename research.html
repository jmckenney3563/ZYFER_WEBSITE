Benchmarking
Methodology Overview

This methodology defines quantitative performance standards across all four of ZYFER's analysis layers — Computer Vision, NLP & Audio Forensics, Behavioral Biometrics, and Network Metadata — plus a unified cross-modal fusion benchmark. It is integrated directly into ZYFER's three-phase deployment roadmap.

// Phase 01 — Weeks 1–4
Pilot Deployment Benchmarks
Deploy to IT security staff and select executives. Establish baselines for all 15 KPIs under live conditions. Capture false alarm rates, inference latency distributions, and cross-modal consistency scores against known-authentic and known-synthetic sample sets.
Baseline Establishment
// Phase 02 — Weeks 5–10
Feedback & Optimization Cycle
Retrain models on pilot data. Run SHAP and LIME fairness audits across all demographic cohorts — race, gender, age group, accent profile. Optimize confidence thresholds to reduce FPR toward target tier. Bias delta KPIs must clear audit threshold before advancing to Phase 3.
Model Optimization
// Phase 03 — Weeks 11–20
Organization-Wide Scale-Up
Full enterprise rollout with automated risk reporting and Human-in-the-Loop escalation protocols active. Benchmark cross-modal consistency at organizational scale. Validate SIEM integration and forensic log completeness under production load across AWS, Azure, and GCP.
Production Validation
// Ongoing — Post Week 20
Continuous Monitoring
Quarterly adversarial retraining cycles, model drift detection, and demographic bias re-audits. Annual governance framework review aligned to NIST AI RMF and ISO/IEC 42001. Dataset refresh to incorporate samples from newly released generative models.
Operational Continuity

// Section 03

Detection Modalities

ZYFER benchmarks each detection layer independently before validating cross-modal fusion. No single modality is sufficient — the system's integrity depends on all four operating within threshold simultaneously.

CV
Computer Vision
CNN and vision transformer analysis of facial biometrics, micro-expression consistency, gaze and blink patterns, landmark geometry, and frame-level compression artifacts. Benchmarked against GAN face-swaps, lip-sync injection, and adversarial landmark correction attacks per Javed et al. (2025).
NLP
NLP & Audio Forensics
Spectral energy distribution analysis, prosody and cadence evaluation, acoustic timbre fingerprinting, and conversational manipulation detection. Covers voice cloning, TTS artifacts, and voice conversion attacks across diverse accent and demographic profiles per Zhang et al. (2025).
BEH
Behavioral Biometrics
Keystroke dynamics, interaction pacing, decision-making urgency patterns, and social engineering linguistic cue detection. Evaluated against automated agent simulations and expert human social engineering scenarios targeting manufactured urgency and authority exploitation patterns.
NET
Network Metadata
IP origin pattern analysis, latency spike signatures consistent with synthetic media streaming, compression artifact profiles, and abnormal packet structures. Benchmarked against curated datasets of known deepfake delivery infrastructure signatures and spoofed connection indicators.
// Section 04

Scoring Rubric & Alert Protocol

All KPI results map to a five-tier scoring system. Each tier triggers a specific operational response, ensuring detection quality degradation is addressed before reaching user-facing impact.

Exceeds Target
KPI meets or exceeds target threshold across all demographic cohorts. No corrective action required.
Meets Baseline
KPI meets baseline but has not reached target. Optimization recommended within 30 days.
Below Baseline
KPI falls between baseline and critical fail. Escalate to retraining team. HITL mandatory for all detections.
Critical Fail
KPI breaches critical threshold. Automated actions suspended. Immediate human override required. Incident report filed.
Audit Required
Demographic bias delta exceeds tolerance. Full SHAP/LIME audit triggered. Deployment paused for affected cohort.
// Section 05

Adversarial Test Protocols

ZYFER's reinforcement learning architecture requires benchmarking under adversarial conditions to validate model robustness against the two primary attack vectors in its threat model: data poisoning and model evasion.

// Protocol 01
Data Poisoning Resistance
Injection of mislabeled synthetic samples into the test batch at defined ratios. Measures detection accuracy degradation under deliberate training data corruption — a primary supply chain attack vector for enterprise AI systems.
// Protocol 02
Model Evasion Resistance
GAN-generated samples specifically optimized to defeat ZYFER's published detection patterns. Validates that the model cannot be reverse-engineered by an adversary with knowledge of the detection architecture.
// Protocol 03
Cross-Modal Spoofing
Authentic video paired with synthetic audio, and vice versa, to test the fusion layer's ability to disambiguate mismatched modality signals. Validates the core ZYFER thesis that no single modality is sufficient for reliable detection.
// Protocol 04
Temporal Consistency Attacks
Deepfake video with artificially corrected landmark geometry designed to defeat Computer Vision layer analysis. Tests whether CV-layer evasion propagates through the correlation engine or is caught by NLP and behavioral signal divergence.
